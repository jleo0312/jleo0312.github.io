Quick answers

Before you book

Do you diagnose check-engine lights or unknown problems?

No. I do not offer diagnostic-only appointments. If you already know the repair or failed component, send me the job and I can tell you whether it fits mobile service.

Can you supply parts?

Yes. OEM parts can be supplied when requested. Parts cost is separate from the $110/hour mobile labor rate.

Do you only work on 5VZ-FE Toyotas?

The 5VZ-FE is the specialty and the main focus of this service. Other straightforward Toyota work may be considered depending on the job.

Where can the work be done?

At a safe, legal, reasonably flat location with enough room to work. Some repairs are not appropriate for mobile service.
